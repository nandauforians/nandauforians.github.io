Act 1: The Escape & the First Kill

Scene 1: Opening Festival Song
Appu and Raja perform in a circus show during a colorful Tamil temple festival. The song is peppy and nostalgic. Cut to night—Raja is brutally killed by four thugs.
Scene 2: Appu in Prison
Appu hears about Raja’s death in a newspaper clipping. Tearful, he swears revenge. Begins plotting escape.
Scene 3: The Great Escape
A slapstick sequence with a marching band, an elephant, and a prison inspector distracted by Appu’s ventriloquism. Appu escapes in a washing machine box.
Scene 4: Balram Gets Assigned
Balram Naidu is pulled out of yoga class and given the case. He vows to catch Appu, even if it means skipping his morning filter coffee.
Scene 5: First Kill – Snake Charm
Appu disguises as a vet, tricks the first killer into a snake therapy spa, and replaces heat pads with cobra baskets.
Scene 6: Balram & Assistant Language Debate
Balram argues with his Kannada-speaking rookie about the origins of Tamil and Kannada. Closes scene with: “Nee Kannada sollara, naan Tamizh pesaren... neenum intha sandai mudikalayaaa???”

Act 2: Disguises, Deceptions, and Death

Scene 7: Roobini’s Return
Roobini spots Appu at a roadside tea shop and follows him. After emotional tension, she decides to help him seek revenge.
Scene 8: Tech Trouble
Appu uses Google Maps and Siri in hysterical fashion. Thinks Alexa is spying for Balram. Smashes smart speaker.
Scene 9: Balram in a Spa Raid
Balram raids a spa thinking Appu is hiding there. Finds only yoga mats and oils. Gets facial by mistake.
Scene 10: Second Kill – Treadmill Trap
Appu disguises as a gym trainer. The victim is tricked into a treadmill trap with oil on the belt. Final sprint leads to fatal accident.
Scene 11: Balram’s Tech Briefing
Balram is shown a tablet of facial recognition. He thinks it's Facebook and posts a selfie with “Wanted” suspect.
Scene 12: Third Kill – Drone Strike
Appu uses a hacked drone to drop a speaker on victim's head during a rooftop pool party.
Scene 13: Balram Interrogates a Magician
A magician who once trained Appu is interrogated. Balram ends up being hypnotized and confesses he wanted to be a dancer.

Act 3: Truth, Tears, and Triumph

Scene 14: Final Kill – Mirror Maze
Appu confronts the final killer in a carnival’s mirror maze. A poetic end where reflections confuse the enemy.
Scene 15: Balram Corners Appu
In a dramatic rooftop confrontation, Balram corners Appu. But when Appu tells the truth, Balram pauses.
Scene 16: Roobini's Confession
Roobini defends Appu, admitting she knew everything. Balram, visibly shaken, has a monologue about justice vs. law.
Scene 17: Appu Disappears
Appu uses a street magic trick to vanish before Balram’s eyes.
Scene 18: Media Uproar
News reports show Appu’s vigilante acts have divided public opinion. Balram is interviewed and stays silent.
Scene 19: Balram Reassigned
Balram is sent to a new case in Andhra. Says goodbye to his assistant with: “Tamil Nadu-la naan break kudukaren. Andhra-la naan break aadaren.”
Scene 20: Appu and Roobini at Circus
Appu, Roobini, and a few orphans run a mobile circus. Last act shows a silent nod from Balram watching them from the crowd.
Scene 21: Flashback to Raja
Montage of Appu-Raja childhood moments. Appu breaks down alone near a banyan tree.
Scene 22: Balram's Letter
Balram writes a poetic letter about Appu. It’s left on a window sill of the circus van. Appu reads it and smiles.
Scene 23: Final Song – Vaa Thalaiva
A powerful yet emotional montage song. Lyrics play over scenes of Appu’s circus inspiring kids, and Balram training recruits.
Scene 24: Closing Shot
Final frame splits between Appu's laughing face and Balram’s proud gaze. Fade out with Crazy Mohan-style pun on justice: “Jus this once... Justice won.”
