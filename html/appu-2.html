🎬 Appu vs Balram

A Kamal Haasan Crossover Crime Comedy – Full Screenplay

Act 1: The Escape & the Chase Begins

Scene 1: Jail Escape
Appu, still grieving over his brother Raja’s death, learns that the four sons of the original villain are responsible. One stormy night, he escapes from prison with a cunning trap involving a circus prop.
Scene 2: The Assignment
CBI Officer Balram Naidu is reassigned from semi-retirement to track the high-profile prison escapee. He sees this as a way to reclaim his lost glory.
Scene 3: The First Kill
Appu targets the first killer who is now a reality show judge. He rigs a stage trap during a live show, causing the judge to fall into a magician's disappearing box—permanently.

Act 2: The Hunt Intensifies

Scene 4: Roobini Returns
Appu reunites with Roobini, who is now a widower. She is moved upon learning about Appu’s long-held love and his loss. She secretly becomes his accomplice.
Scene 5: Balram’s Blunders
Balram's attempts to catch Appu often result in slapstick failures. He interviews circus staff and Roobini unknowingly, who mislead him with sharp wit and confusion.
Scene 6: The Second and Third Kills
Appu engineers elaborate murder setups: one involving a treadmill and hologram at a gym, another at a VR gaming zone. These comedic murders keep the tone dark yet hilarious.

Act 3: Revelation, Redemption & Reversal

Scene 7: Emotional Lock-Up
Appu is captured after the third murder. In the jail, he confides in Balram. The emotional story softens Balram who now secretly begins to question the system.
Scene 8: The Final Kill
With Roobini’s help, Appu escapes for one last mission. He corners the final villain at a political event and uses a stage firework trick to deliver justice.
Scene 9: The Closure
Balram lets Appu go under the guise of "technical error" and resigns with a smile. Appu returns to circus life. Roobini joins him. As Appu closes a curtain on stage, he says, “Ippo dhaan nambalukku real applause.”
