📽️ Screenplay Structure – 3 Acts, 24 Scenes
ACT 1: THE TRIGGER (Scenes 1–8)
Tone: Emotional, ironic. Characters are introduced, prison tension builds, and a thrilling escape kicks things into motion.
  • 1. The Death of Raja: Raja is killed in a staged accident. One killer whispers, “Appuvoda payyan da avan.”
  • 2. Prison Life: Appu spars with guards. He’s attacked during a meal, but a mistaken short prisoner is killed. Appu sees a conspiracy.
  • 3. News Break: News of Raja’s death. Appu clenches the paper, “எனக்கு மீண்டும் ஒரு வாய்ப்பு குடுத்திருக்காங்க.”
  • 4. Mirror Monologue: Appu stares into a cracked mirror. "சின்ன height... பெரிய பக்காவா? அவங்கயே காட்டணும்.”
  • 5. Circus Escape: Power outage. Appu fakes a seizure, slips into the sewers using old contortionist tricks.
  • 6. Enter Balram Naidu: Comedic re-entry. Balram fumbles with AI tools, annoyed by modern acronyms. “ChatPT...PT teacher-aa?”
  • 7. Meeting Kutta Mani: Appu seeks help from Kutta Mani. They joke while forging IDs and planning revenge.
  • 8. Clue from the Past: A photo of Raja’s wife (linked to villains) leads Appu down a vengeful path.
ACT 2: THE REVENGE GAME (Scenes 9–17)
Tone: Dark comedy, thrilling kills, rising stakes.
  • 9. Bath Bomb: Ravi’s brother dies in a spa via bath salt bomb. “Bubble bath... burst karma.”
  • 10. Balram’s Realization: Recognizes Appu’s signature style. “Idhu circus style murder!”
  • 11. Mano Returns: Emotional reunion. Old tapes from Raja rekindle fire. “Raja’s death wasn’t an accident.”
  • 12. Golf Drone Kill: Ravi survives the first drone attempt but gets hit while mocking Appu. “Fore... and gone.”
  • 13. Balram’s Dilemma: Discusses Appu’s justice with himself in a mirror. “Law-la irundhaalum, idhu unga brother da.”
  • 14. Hideout Busted: Kutta Mani is caught. Appu barely escapes, crawling through shadows.
  • 15. Flashback Montage: Raja’s marriage, betrayal, his wife’s secret support, tragic love lost.
  • 16. Final Villain Sets Trap: Ravi Sethupathi prepares a trap for Appu, using Mano as bait.
  • 17. Mano Caught: Appu saves Mano but is caught. A slow clap from Balram ends the scene.
ACT 3: THE FINAL JOKE (Scenes 18–24)
Tone: Tragicomic end, social commentary, poetic closure.
  • 18. Interrogation: Balram questions Appu. “Justice-na enna nu sollunga sir... naan purinjukaren.”
  • 19. Media Uproar: Appu becomes a social phenomenon. Twitter hashtags trend: #JusticeByAppu
  • 20. Verdict: Court montage. Appu is sentenced to death.
  • 21. Final Night: Mano visits Appu. Quiet bonding. “En height-ku oru chair thara sollunga.”
  • 22. Gallows Prep: Guards argue rope length. Comic touches amid tension.
  • 23. Hanging Scene: Appu swings gently. Feet don’t quite touch the floor. Haunting silence.
  • 24. Balram Alone: He visits Appu’s cell. Sees etched line: “Justice, not revenge.” Silent tears. Fade out.
